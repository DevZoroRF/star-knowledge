Data deletion instructions

Star Knowledge · last updated 9 November 2026

The Star Knowledge app does not collect or store personal data about viewers, so in most cases there is nothing to delete.

If you believe we hold any data about you, or you would like us to remove a comment or anything else related to you, email devzoro@yahoo.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will reply and delete it within 30 days.

You can also remove the Star Knowledge app's access at any time in your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

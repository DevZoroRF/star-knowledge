Privacy policy

Star Knowledge · last updated 9 November 2026

Star Knowledge makes short animated videos about how everyday things work and publishes them on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. This page explains what our publishing tool does with data.

What our app does

The "Star Knowledge" app is a private tool used only by the Star Knowledge team. It uploads our own videos, titles and descriptions to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and reads the view, like and comment counts of those videos so we can see how they perform.

What we collect from viewers

Nothing. The app does not let anyone log in, does not collect names, emails or other personal information from viewers, and does not use cookies or tracking. We only see the same totals (views, likes, comments) that each platform shows to every page owner.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

Children

Our videos are made for a general audience of curious people of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Platforms

When you watch our videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook, those platforms' own privacy policies apply to your use of their services.
